Privacy Policy

Static Mind and Static Mind uploader · Last updated 16 November 2026

Who we are

Static Mind publishes short psychology videos on Instagram and YouTube. "Static Mind uploader" (the "App") is a private tool used only by the owner of the Static Mind YouTube channel to upload Static Mind's own videos to that channel.

YouTube API Services

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using the App you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data the App accesses

  • Upload access (scope youtube.upload): used to upload videos, with their title, description, tags and settings, to the signed-in owner's own channel.
  • Read-only channel access (scope youtube.readonly): used to confirm which channel is connected and to check that an uploaded video finished processing.

The App does not read, collect or store data about viewers, subscribers, commenters or any other YouTube user.

How data is stored

The only data the App keeps is the OAuth refresh token that Google issues when the owner signs in. It is stored locally on the owner's own computer and is used only to upload videos. Nothing is stored on any server, and no cookies or analytics are used by the App.

Sharing

The App does not sell, share, or transfer any data to third parties. Data is sent only to Google, as needed to upload videos through the YouTube Data API.

Deleting data and revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page. Revoking access makes the stored token useless; the owner can also delete it from their computer. Uploaded videos can be deleted in YouTube Studio.

Website

This website is a static page. It does not use cookies, analytics or forms, and it collects no personal data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
